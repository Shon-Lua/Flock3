as one.

The hardware, device firmware and desktop companion each have a distinct role. Together, they form the Flock3 ecosystem.

Flock3+

Flock3+ is firmware for an online music player. It lets you listen to music online for free, download tracks and organize your own playlists. It supports MP3 and WAV playback on the 4.7-inch E-Paper display of a device manufactured by Shenzhen Xinyuan Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.

Flock3 Hub

Flock3 Hub is a Windows desktop music player that lets you listen to music online for free, download tracks, and organize your own playlists. It supports MP3 and WAV audio playback on your PC. The app is currently in development.
